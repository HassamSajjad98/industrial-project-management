Methodology & limitations

CPM uses finish-to-start dependencies without lags or resource constraints. CPI = EV / AC; SPI = EV / PV; EAC = BAC / CPI assumes cost efficiency persists; VAC = BAC − EAC. SPI is not a direct forecast of finish date. All values are synthetic and are not employer data.
